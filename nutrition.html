MEAL 1

20-Minute Creamy Tomato Salmon Skillet

Active Time:
20 mins

Total Time:
20 mins

Servings:
4

Ingredients

  • 0.6 kg salmon fillet, skinned and cut into 4 portions
  • ¼ teaspoon salt, divided
  • ¼ teaspoon ground pepper, divided
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 medium zucchini, halved lengthwise and thinly sliced
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • ⅓ cup dry white wine
  • 425 grams can no-salt-added diced tomatoes
  • 57 grams cream cheese, cut into cubes
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh basil

Directions


  1. Step 1 Pat salmon dry and sprinkle with 1/8 teaspoon each salt and pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the salmon and cook until the underside is browned and releases easily from the pan, 3 to 4 minutes. Flip the salmon and continue to cook until opaque in the centre, another 2 to 3 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
  2. Step 2
    Meanwhile, add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil, zucchini and onion to the pan. Cook, stirring, until starting to soften, about 3 minutes. Increase the heat to medium-high and add wine. Cook, stirring, until the liquid has mostly evaporated, about 2 minutes. Add tomatoes, cream cheese, Italian seasoning, garlic powder and the remaining 1/8 teaspoon each salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring, until the cream cheese is melted, 4 to 5 minutes. Return the salmon to the pan and turn to coat with the sauce. Serve topped with basil.

MEAL 2

Loaded Bean & Broccoli Salad

Active Time:
25 mins

Total Time:
25 mins

Servings:
4

Ingredients

  • 5 slices bacon, chopped
  • ⅓ cup whole-milk plain strained yoghurt, such as Greek-style
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise
  • 2½ tablespoons cider vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
  • ¾ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¾ teaspoon onion powder
  • ¼ teaspoon ground pepper, plus more for garnish
  • 4 cups broccoli florets (½-inch)
  • 1 (15-ounce) can no-salt-added cannellini beans, rinsed
  • ¾ cup shredded sharp Cheddar cheese
  • 5 medium scallions, thinly sliced

Directions


  1. Step 1 Cook chopped bacon in a large skillet over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until crisp, about 6 minutes. Transfer to a paper towel–lined plate. Measure 1 tablespoon of the drippings into a large bowl; discard the remaining drippings or reserve for another use.
  2. Step 2
    Add ⅓ cup yogurt, ⅓ cup mayonnaise, 2½ tablespoons vinegar, 2 tablespoons dill, ¾ teaspoon garlic powder, ¾ teaspoon onion powder and ¼ teaspoon pepper to the bowl with the drippings; whisk until combined. Add 4 cups broccoli, the rinsed cannellini beans, ¾ cup Cheddar and the cooked bacon and all but 2 tablespoons of the sliced scallions; toss until evenly coated. Top with the remaining 2 tablespoons scallions. Garnish with additional pepper, if desired.

MEAL 3

Honey-Mustard Chicken & Broccoli Casserole Is the Perfect High-Protein Dinner

Active Time:
25 mins

Total Time:
40 mins

Servings:
6

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces whole-wheat pasta shells
  • 6 cups bite-size broccoli florets
  • 2½ cups half-and-half
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 2 teaspoons cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon dry mustard
  • 1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme, plus more leaves for garnish
  • ½ teaspoon salt plus ⅛ teaspoon, divided
  • ¼ teaspoon ground pepper
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken breast
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • ⅓ cup panko breadcrumbs, preferably whole-wheat

Directions


  1. Step 1 Preheat oven to 375°F. Lightly coat a 9-by-13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
  2. Step 2
    Bring a large pot of water to a boil over high heat. Add 12 ounces of pasta; cook, stirring occasionally, until just tender, 4 to 5 minutes. Add 6 cups of broccoli; continue cooking until the broccoli is bright green, about 1½ minutes. Drain well.
  3. Step 3
    In the prepared baking dish, whisk 2½ cups half-and-half, ½ cup Parmesan, 2 tablespoons mustard, 1 tablespoon honey, 2 teaspoons cornstarch, 1 teaspoon each onion powder, garlic powder, dry mustard and fresh thyme, ½ teaspoon salt and ¼ teaspoon pepper until smooth. Stir in the pasta mixture and 2 cups chicken until evenly combined.
  4. Step 4
    Cover tightly with foil and bake until the sauce is slightly thickened, about 20 minutes. Remove the foil and stir until the pasta is evenly coated.
  5. Step 5
    Meanwhile, melt 1 tablespoon butter in a small skillet over medium heat. Add ⅓ cup panko and the remaining ⅛ teaspoon salt; cook, stirring constantly, until golden brown, about 3 minutes. Remove from heat.
  6. Step 6
    Sprinkle the casserole with the toasted panko and 1 tablespoon chives. Garnish with additional thyme leaves, if desired

MEAL 4

Loaded Bean & Broccoli Salad

Active Time:
20 mins

Total Time:
20 mins

Servings:
2

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup whole-milk plain strained (Greek-style) yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh tarragon
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh chives
  • •1 tablespoon mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • 1 teaspoon drained nonpareil capers
  • 1 medium clove of garlic, minced
  • ¼ teaspoon salt plus ⅛ teaspoon, divided
  • 1 cup cooked white quinoa
  • 1 (15-ounce) can no-salt-added chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 medium carrot, peeled and shaved into ribbons
  • 1 cup thinly sliced red cabbage
  • ½ cup thinly sliced scallions
  • ½ medium avocado, sliced

Directions


  1. Step 1 Combine ¼ cup yogurt, 2 tablespoons parsley, 1 tablespoon each tarragon, chives, mayonnaise, lemon juice and water, 1 teaspoon capers, the minced garlic and ¼ teaspoon salt in a mini food processor. Process until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed, about 1 minute.
  2. Step 2
    Divide 1 cup quinoa between 2 bowls. Top with the rinsed chickpeas, carrot ribbons, 1 cup cabbage, ½ cup scallions and the sliced avocado. Sprinkle each bowl with a pinch of salt. Drizzle with the dressing.
